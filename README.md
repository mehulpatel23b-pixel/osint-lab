## Roadmap

| Phase | Module | Skills | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Foundations](modules/01-foundations/) | threat models, OPSEC, documentation | 1 week |
| 2 | [Domain Recon](modules/02-domain-recon/) | WHOIS, DNS, subdomains | 1 week |
| 3 | [Web OSINT](modules/03-web-osint/) | dorks, archives, fingerprinting | 1 week |
| 4 | [Metadata & Geo](modules/04-metadata-geo/) | EXIF, image forensics, geolocation | 1 week |
| 5 | [People & Usernames](modules/05-people-usernames/) | username enum, verification | 1 week |
| 6 | [Infrastructure](modules/06-infrastructure/) | Shodan, Censys, exposed services | 2 weeks |
| 7 | [Build Your Own Tools](modules/07-automation-python/) | Python OSINT tooling | ongoing |

Start at module 01. Track your progress in [progress.md](progress.md).

## Ground rules

Read [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md) first. The short version:
- **Your own assets, or sanctioned labs. Nothing else.**
- Skills built on CTF platforms (TryHackMe, HackTheBox, Trace Labs) transfer 1:1.
- The best OSINT practitioners are the ones with clean records.
