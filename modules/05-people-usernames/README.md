# Module 05 — Usernames & Digital Footprints

Practice target: **your own usernames.**

## Concepts
- Username enumeration across platforms (people reuse handles)
- Cross-referencing: registration dates, writing style, photo backgrounds
- Verification: a real analyst never trusts one source

## Tools
```bash
pip install sherlock-project
sherlock your_handle          # your handle, not someone else's
```
- [WhatsMyName](https://whatsmyname.app)

## Exercise
1. Run sherlock on your own primary username. Log every hit.
2. Find accounts of yours you forgot existed. Delete the ones you do not use.
3. Check your old usernames on Wayback — did deleted profiles survive in archives?
4. Turn it around: pick a public company's official handle (companies are fair
   game — their brand footprint is public business info) and map its footprint.

**Done when:** you know exactly what your username says about you across the internet.
