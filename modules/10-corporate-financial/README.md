# Module 10 — Corporate & Financial OSINT (Advanced)

Business intelligence from public registries. Fair game: registered companies
are public by design. Best first target: **a company you'd like to sell a
website to** — this module doubles as lead research for your SiteSeller work.

## Concepts
- Company registries: India MCA (mca.gov.in), OpenCorporates, SEC EDGAR (US)
- GSTIN / CIN verification — the official record behind a business card
- Directors' public filings, trademark registries (ipindia.gov.in)
- Merging registries into a company profile

## Exercise
1. Pick a local business. Verify its GSTIN via the public GST portal.
2. Look it up on OpenCorporates / MCA — registered address, directors, age.
3. Check its trademark filings on ipindia.gov.in. Anything pending?
4. Cross-check: does the registered address match its Google Maps listing?
5. Assemble a 1-page lead profile (registered name, status, decision-makers, digital gaps).
   Bonus: this profile is exactly what your SiteSeller outreach needs.

**Done when:** you can verify any Indian business's legal identity from public registries in under 10 minutes.
