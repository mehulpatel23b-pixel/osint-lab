# Module 08 — GEOINT & Imagery Analysis (Advanced)

Bellingcat-style visual verification. Practice targets: photos **you** took, or
published satellite imagery / CTF challenges (e.g., GeoGuessr study runs).

## Concepts
- Geolocation workflow: extract features (terrain, buildings, signage, sun position) → form hypotheses → confirm
- Sun angle & shadow analysis (SunCalc) to verify time and hemisphere
- Reverse image search as a verification tool (Yandex, Google Lens)
- Open satellite layers: Sentinel Hub EO Browser (free), Google Earth historical imagery

## Exercise
1. Take a photo outdoors. Before checking EXIF, note 5 locatable features.
2. Use SunCalc to check: does the shadow direction in your photo match your location + time?
3. Reverse-search your photo. Now repeat with a stock photo of a famous landmark — can you find the original spot?
4. Open Sentinel Hub EO Browser over your city. Identify your neighborhood from imagery alone, no labels.
5. CTF practice: GeoGuessr 5 rounds — after each, write down which features gave the location away.

**Done when:** you can justify a geolocation with evidence, not a guess.
