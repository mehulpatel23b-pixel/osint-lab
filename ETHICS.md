# Ethics & Legal Boundaries

OSINT is powerful, and in India (and most countries) the line between
"publicly available information" and crimes like stalking or unauthorized
access is thin. This lab stays firmly on the legal side.

## Rules for this repo

1. **Only investigate:**
   - Assets you own (your domains, servers, social accounts)
   - Sanctioned CTF/lab targets (TryHackMe, HackTheBox, Trace Labs, picoCTF)
   - People who have given you explicit permission
2. **Never** track, profile, or monitor a person without consent — regardless
   of what a tool makes technically possible.
3. **Never** access systems without authorization. Scanning is not harmless.
4. Log out of everything you open. An OSINT session should leave zero trace
   on the target and your own machine keeps the notes.

## Relevant law (India)

- Information Technology Act, 2000 — Sections 43, 66, 66C, 66D (unauthorized
  access, identity theft, cheating by impersonation)
- IPC/BNS provisions on stalking and privacy

If you are unsure whether an action is legal, it is out of scope for this lab.
